{# Student board markup. Included by kiosk.html (pinned /kiosk/student and /kiosk/demo) and kiosk_combined.html. Parts are addressed by data- attributes scoped to the board root, not global ids, so this can share a document with the mentor board on the combined page. Context: teams, by_team, and optionally demo, has_camera_hud. The camera shares the Leaderboard's column, stacked below it, instead of floating over a roster panel or claiming a 4th column of its own — both earlier layouts either had to permanently shrink Team 4143's panel to keep an overlaid HUD clear of it, or made the board feel four-wide when the camera isn't really a peer of Team 4143/4423/Leaderboard. The Leaderboard has spare vertical room (it's a short list), so it's a natural host: .board-col-stack turns that column into its own small flex column, giving Leaderboard the stretchy share and the camera panel just what its content needs. `demo` renders a non-functional outline in the same spot instead, so /kiosk/demo previews the real layout without ever requesting camera access — only the combined display (kiosk_combined.html) actually runs one (see kiosk.py's kiosk_page vs kiosk_demo). #camera-hud is a single live element (one video stream, one qr-scanner instance) — it's never duplicated onto the mentor board. When the combined display swaps to show mentors instead, kiosk_combined.html's show() physically relocates it into _board_mentor.html's own [data-camera-slot] (an appendChild move, which doesn't interrupt an already-running stream) so it keeps working and stays visible no matter which board is up, then moves it back on the way to students. #}
